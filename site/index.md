A project to keep the Osborne 1 alive: a Z80 bus interposer that adds
modern peripherals to the original machine without modifying it, the
reverse-engineering that makes that possible — and, downstream, a
modern-parts reproduction of the whole computer.

This blog is the story around the engineering: the machines, the mods,
the people, and the build log.

- Code, research, and hardware definitions:
  [github.com/Myrcurial/o1nextgen](https://github.com/Myrcurial/o1nextgen)
